# Custom / Decompiled Pandanda Rooms

---

All Pandanda rooms included in this repo have been fully decompiled and publish settings setup in a way to allow you to instantly publish the rooms, I would recommend using the below software:

## Small tweaks and tests (Not reliable works on SWF only)
JPEX Flash Decompiler

## Full FLA editing
Adobe Animate 2024 or higher
[https://www.adobe.com/products/animate.html](https://www.adobe.com/products/animate.html)

Once you open it in Animate you can make your adjustments and then click publish, any changes to the AS you make in the rooms folder when you publish is automatically packaged into the file.

These files have been decompiled by @poitor and the working solution to decompiling allowing working publishing of the files for use again in SFS / Pandanda was discovered by @poitor & iari, This had to be manually re-discovered due to the information being gate kept.


## Important Notes

When removing assets or symbols from existing scenes ensure you update all necessary AS files that may reference those symbols or assets otherwise your room will fail when doing debug testing in Animate or when attempting to load it in Pandanda.
